# Northwind Example Bank: Consumer Account Opening KYC Standard (SYNTHETIC)

Synthetic policy for the QA smoke test. Northwind Example Bank, its staff and every rule below are invented.
Version 1.0, effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Financial Crime Compliance (fictional).

## 1. Scope

This standard applies to every new consumer deposit account opened online or in a branch in the United States.

## 2. Customer identification

2.1 The analyst must verify the identity of every account owner before the account is opened.

2.2 For an owner under 18, the analyst must verify the identity of the minor and of the adult who opens the account with them.

2.3 The analyst must not accept an expired government photo ID as identity evidence.

## 3. Address

3.1 The analyst must confirm the residential address with a utility statement or bank statement issued within the last 90 days.

## 4. Sanctions screening

4.1 The analyst must screen every owner against the sanctions list before the account is opened.

4.2 A similar name alone must not be treated as a confirmed sanctions match; the analyst must compare date of birth and address.

## 5. Information requests

5.1 When information is missing, the analyst must send one specific request that names the missing item, the reason and a deadline of 10 business days.

## 6. Recommendation

6.1 The analyst may recommend approval only when every check in sections 2 to 5 is complete. The final decision is made by an authorised officer.
