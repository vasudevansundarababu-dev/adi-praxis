# 7. Expert (SME)

This chapter is for subject-matter experts who write tasks on ADI Praxis. You use the **candidate app**. The journey:

```
Sign in ─► Verify identity ─► Apply (opportunity, careers page or partner invite) ─► Build your SME profile
   ─► A Validator decides ─► Onboarding ─► (Course and certification test, for requisitions)
   ─► Accept an assignment ─► Write tasks ─► A Validator accepts them ─► Get paid weekly
```

Your **Home** page shows your progress ("Verify identity · Build profile · Reviewer decision · Onboarding · Assignment
· Write tasks") and a **Next step** card that always tells you what to do now.

## 7.1 Sign in

1. Open the candidate app and select **Sign in**. You see **"Sign in to ADI Praxis"**.
2. The first time, tick **"I confirm I am 18 or over."**
3. Select **Continue with Google**.
   - **Test and demo environments:** a yellow box "Demo sign-in (simulated Google)" asks for **Email \*** and **Name
     \***. Use a new email for each new candidate you test. Select **Continue with Google (mock)**.
4. ADI Praxis only asks Google for your sign-in; it never reads Gmail, Drive or contacts.

## 7.2 Verify your identity

You must verify your identity before you submit a profile or open tasks. It protects the contributor community; it
does not verify your qualifications.

1. On **Verify your identity**, select **Start verification**.
2. Photograph an identity document with the provider (Stripe Identity), and a selfie if asked. ADI Praxis never stores
   your document images, ID number or date of birth.
3. The page updates by itself: Processing → **Identity verified**. Select **Continue**.

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Continue verification" | The check needs more (for example the document expired). Try again or ask for help |
| "Canceled" | Start again, or ask for help |
| "Help needed" | Our team is reviewing your case and will contact you |

**I need help**: choose the problem (document type not accepted, country not supported, I do not consent to the
automated check, it keeps failing, something else) → **Ask for help**. A Validator can verify you by an approved
alternative process.

**Test environment:** a page "Simulate the Stripe Identity result" lets you choose the outcome: **Verified (18 or
over)**, Verified but under 18, Needs more input, Processing, Canceled.

## 7.3 Find work and apply

There are three ways in:

- **Opportunities** (menu): open one and read it: what you'll need (Required and Preferred criteria), time commitment,
  what not to share. Select **Apply: build my SME profile →**.
- **A careers page link** for a requisition: read the role, sign in, fill **Your relevant experience \***, **Years of
  relevant experience \***, qualifications, languages, **Country \***, tick the consent box → **Apply and continue →**.
- **A partner invite email** (a staffing partner put you forward): open the link and sign in with the same email the
  partner used. The application opens. Invites last 7 days.

## 7.4 Build your SME profile

The profile is a short chat: up to six questions, most answered with a tap. It saves as you go.

1. Answer each question by tapping the chips that apply and/or typing, then **Send**. If you don't know or can't say,
   use **I don't know** or **I can't say**. Optional questions have **Skip**.
2. Keep examples general: **no customer names, account or ID numbers.** If you type something sensitive it is removed
   and you are asked to describe it in general terms.
3. The **Your SME profile** panel (beside the chat, or a tab on a phone) lists what was picked up from your answers.
   **Confirm** each item, or **Edit** or **Not right, remove**. Nothing counts until you confirm it.
4. Add your roles with dates (**Role title \***, From, To) and any credentials (for example CAMS). Credentials stay
   "self-reported" until a Validator checks the evidence.

Prefer a form? Select **Prefer a form? Answer the same questions here**.

## 7.5 Upload evidence and submit

1. Select **Review and submit**. **Before you submit** lists anything still to confirm or answer.
2. Under **Supporting evidence**, tick "I have permission to share these files for qualification review", then upload
   only what supports your key claims (a redacted role letter, a public credential listing). **PDF, Word, PNG or JPG,
   up to 10 MB.** Never upload customer data, full payslips, ID documents or passwords. Attach each file to a
   credential if you like.
3. Tick the two required statements and select **Confirm and submit**. It stays disabled until everything is confirmed
   and your identity is verified.

You see **"Profile submitted"**. A person reviews every application.

## 7.6 Follow your application

Open the application from Home to see its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| With a Validator | Being reviewed; nothing to do |
| More information requested | Read **What we need**, select **Update my profile**, answer and submit again |
| Selected | See **Your approved scope**, then **Start onboarding** |
| Not selected this time | Read the message. **Ask for a review** if something was missed: "What should the Validator know?" → **Request review**. A different person looks again |

You can **Withdraw this application** at any time; your history is kept.

## 7.7 Onboarding

A checklist you can finish in several sittings:

1. **Confirm your approved scope** → **This is correct**
2. **Contributor terms** → **I have read and accept these terms**
3. **Confidentiality and IP** → accept
4. **Data use** → accept
5. **Conflicts and authorisation**: say whether you have an employer or customer conflict (describe it briefly, without
   confidential details) and confirm you will contribute only original or permitted material → **Save**
6. **Task guidance**: how tasks are structured, synthetic data only, another expert reviews every task → **I understand**
7. **Work preferences**: country, languages and level, time zone, hours a week, proposal emails → **Save**

For requisition roles two more steps tick themselves: **Guidelines course and certification** (section 7.8) and
**Payment set-up** (section 7.12).

## 7.8 Course and certification (requisition roles)

**Certifications** (menu) lists your certifications with their status.

**The course**

1. **Start the course →**. Modules open one after another.
2. Read each module, try the **Practice** items (they don't count), then take the quiz. **Get at least 80% right** to
   finish the module; you can retry straight away.

**The test**

1. **Go to the test →**. Read **Before you start**: the test is timed, answers save as you go, pasting and leaving the
   page are logged for reviewers (they never fail anyone on their own), and you have up to 3 attempts.
2. **Take the selfie check →** before every attempt. Only the result is kept. (Simulated in test.)
3. **Start the test now →**. A countdown stays on screen (yellow under 5 minutes). Questions can be: choose one, choose
   all that apply, put steps in order, tick steps that break the guidelines, or a written answer.
4. **Review and submit** → **Submit test** → **Yes, submit my test**. When time runs out, your saved answers are
   submitted.

**Results** show **You passed**, **You didn't pass this time**, or **Your test is being reviewed by a person**, with a
band per section (Strong, Meets, Developing, Below). Questions and answers are not shown. After a fail you can retake
**after 48 hours with new questions**. After 3 attempts a person reviews your certification. Certificates last 12
months.

If the role needs a **professional licence**, upload **Licence number \***, **Issuing body \*** and the certificate
(PDF, PNG or JPG, up to 10 MB). A Validator checks it before you can be certified.

## 7.9 Accept an assignment

When a project needs you, a **proposal** appears on Home under **Assignments**: domain, your role, what you'll produce,
effort per task, deadline, compensation and confidentiality. Reply by the date shown (7 days).

- **Accept**, or **Decline** with an optional reason (it won't count against you).
- After accepting, select **Open workbench**. You see your project's task list.

## 7.10 Write a KYC task

On the project page, select **Get next task**. (You can hold up to 3 open tasks.) Open the task. The **workbench**
has sections W00–W06 on the left; everything saves automatically ("All changes save automatically" / "Saved"). The time
target (TPT) is for planning and pay; your task never expires.

> **Everything must be invented.** Never use real customers, colleagues, employers or documents.

**W00 — How do you want to start?** The assistant can write a first draft of every section. You check and confirm each
one; a Validator still decides.

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Answer a short interview** | Eight quick questions (the case, onboarding or refresh, red flags, checks in order, the ambiguous result, the right outcome, risk rating, what a junior would miss) |
| **Start from a document** | Upload your own procedure notes: PDF, Word or text, up to 10 MB |
| **Start from an interview transcript** | PDF, Word, text, or captions (.vtt, .srt), up to 10 MB |
| **Write it myself** | Go straight to W01 |

For uploads, tick "This file contains no customer data, and I am allowed to share it…". Up to 5 files per task. If a
file seems to contain real identifiers you are warned, line by line. Then select **Draft my task** (about a minute;
you can leave and come back). **Draft again** replaces only the sections you have not confirmed.

**Drafted sections** show **AI draft: check and confirm**. Read every field, fix anything wrong or unrealistic, then
select **I've checked this section**. **Redraft** with an optional note ("e.g. make the near match a trustee, not a
director") replaces that section. You cannot submit until every drafted section is confirmed.

**The sections**

| Section | What you write |
| --- | --- |
| **W01 Slot brief** | Your slot (brief, primary work, verification, human gate, tools). Title, difficulty 1–5, jurisdiction, language. For an open slot, tag your case and name the edge case. **See an example task** shows a worked example |
| **W02 Scenario and customer file** | Trigger (new onboarding or periodic refresh), customer type, business context, **parties** (use **Generate synthetic name and address**; identifiers use `SYN-` formats), ownership links, purpose, expected activity |
| **W03 Evidence and recorded tool calls** | Documents (with deliberate defects), the tools available to the agent, and a **response for each tool** — the arguments the agent sends and the result the environment returns, recorded as an MCP call so RL Gym can replay it exactly. The synthetic policy |
| **W04 Reference trajectory** | The steps an ideal agent takes (each: action, tool, the recorded call, expected result, why it matters), findings, the disposition (approve, reject, request information, escalate), risk rating, rationale, and the **hand-off to a human**, which is required for approve, reject or high risk |
| **W05 Rubric and failure modes** | Criteria with weights totalling 100 and at least one critical, testable pass conditions, plausible wrong paths and which criterion catches each |

Live checks under each section show ✓ or • (for example "Rubric weights must sum to 100", "Owners of X total 80%, not
100%", at least 6 steps from difficulty 3).

**W06 Check and submit**

1. **Run checks**. Fix anything listed; links take you to the section.
2. **Possible real data**: replace each flagged value with a synthetic one, or explain why it is not real (the
   Validator sees your explanation).
3. **Closest existing tasks**: make your case clearly different from these.
4. Tick "This case is entirely synthetic…" and select **Submit for review**.

The task becomes read-only and shows "Submitted · awaiting review".

> On a phone the workbench is read-only. Use a laptop or desktop.

## 7.11 Rework

If the Validator asks for changes, the task shows **Changes requested**. Open it: a yellow box "The Validator asked for
changes" lists each comment with a link to its section. Fix them and submit again through W06. If a task is not
accepted, you see the reason.

## 7.12 Payments

**Payments** (menu):

1. **Set up payments →** opens the payout provider's secure page in a new tab, where you add a tax form and payout
   method. ADI Praxis never sees your bank or tax details. (Test environment: a "Simulated payout provider" page; select
   **Finish and return to ADI Praxis →**.)
2. Back on Payments, **Check my status** until it shows **Ready**.
3. You earn for each task a person accepts; it appears the same day. Payouts go out weekly once your unpaid balance
   reaches USD 10 or INR 500.

If a partner put you forward and pays you, payment set-up shows "Not needed".
