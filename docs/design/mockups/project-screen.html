      3 · The task they would author (SME cheat sheet)

      Each expert writes one or more KYC cases as a reusable task in a fixed form, using made-up customers only:

      S1 AssignmentThe case in one line, what triggers it, and its scope. Example: a joint checking account where one applicant's licence has expired.
      S2 InputsThe synthetic applicants, documents and product.
      S3–S4 Tools and rulesWhich bank tools are used, and which federal and state rules apply.
      S5 StepsWhat a careful KYC officer does, step by step, and what happens if a check fails.
      S6–S8 Outcome and scoringThe right decision (for example "request information"), 20 scoring criteria, sources.

      Good fits have done this work themselves: they can explain a real (anonymised) case step by step and say which rule drove each decision.

      4 · What to send back for each candidate

      First name, last name, emailRequired. The email must be the candidate's Gmail address; it becomes their sign-in.
      Assessment against the JDFor each requirement in section 2: met or not, with the evidence you checked.
      Why they fitA few sentences in your words.
      Answers to the questionsThe candidate's answers to the six questions in the sheet.
      OptionalLinkedIn URL and a CV (PDF).

      Fill in the attached sheet, one row per candidate, and send it back with any CVs. Never include real customer or employer data.
